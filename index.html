About Us

The gap between the rendering and the reality.

GREY builds in a market that has learned to distrust promises — where projects are sold on a rendering and delivered on a compromise. What we offer is not square metres, but the confidence that the specification survives contact with the site: the finish schedule signed in the sales office is the one installed on handover, on the date given.

Our Commitments

Three obligations we hold ourselves to on every development — the measure of whether a project was built to the standard, or merely sold at it.

Engineered

Specified for the second owner. Materials are chosen for how they age, not how they photograph — built to outlast the financing behind them.

Documented

Every figure is calculated before it is published, and every variation is raised early — in writing, with the cost attached.

Delivered

A programme measured, not hoped for. The handover date is a calculation the whole team is accountable to — not a marketing promise.
