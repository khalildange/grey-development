About Us

Built the way engineers would build for themselves.

GREY Development Limited is a realty, construction and investment company serving discerning homeowners, institutional investors and public- and private-sector partners across Nigeria. We treat architectural precision as non-negotiable — handling real estate, construction and development as a single discipline, not three handoffs. We do not build to impress at handover; we build to still perform, and still impress, twenty years after handover.

Our Vision

To be West Africa's clearest reference point for what disciplined, architecturally serious development looks like.

Our Mission

To design, build and deliver residential and mixed-use developments of uncompromising structural and material integrity — on schedule, on specification, every time.
